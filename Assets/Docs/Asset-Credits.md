# Delivery Guy - Asset Credits

This file records third-party visual, audio, and font assets included in or used by the game. Accessed 11 September 2026.

## Visual assets

### City Pack - Top Down - Pixel Art
- Creator: NYKNCK
- Use: city roads, pavements, buildings, water, props, scenery, and vehicle artwork.
- Source: https://nyknck.itch.io/citypackpixelart
- Changes: tiles were arranged, layered, and resized in Unity; simplified road tiles were also used for the minimap.

### Complete UI Essential Pack
- Creator: Crusenho
- Use: menu panels, buttons, frames, bars, speech bubbles, and HUD elements.
- Source: https://crusenho.itch.io/complete-ui-essential-pack
- Changes: sprites were imported, sliced, resized, recoloured where needed, and arranged into the UI.

### Icons Essential Pack
- Creator: Crusenho
- Use: interface icons, including sound and other controls where present.
- Source: https://crusenho.itch.io/icons-essential-pack-free-icons
- Changes: individual icons were selected, resized, and placed in the UI.

### Pocket GUI
- Creator: Vryell
- Use: smartphone-style phone interface and related UI elements.
- Source: https://vryell.itch.io/pocket-gui
- Changes: phone and menu elements were resized and arranged in Unity.

### Pokemon Style Characters - Townspeople
- Creator: Screen Smith
- Use: pedestrian NPC sprites and animations.
- Source: https://screensmith.itch.io/pokemon-style-characters-townspeople
- Changes: selected characters were configured as animated NPCs in Unity.

### Pokemon Style Characters - Miners and Construction Workers
- Creator: Screen Smith
- Use: additional pedestrian NPC sprites and animations.
- Creator page: https://screensmith.itch.io/
- Changes: selected characters were configured as animated NPCs in Unity.

### Pixel Dimensional Portal 32x32
- Creator: Pixelnauta
- Use: animated delivery drop-off portals.
- Source: https://pixelnauta.itch.io/pixel-dimensional-portal-32x32
- Changes: the spritesheet was sliced and animated in Unity; its scale and/or colour may have been adjusted.

## Font

### Minecraft
- Creator: Craftron Gaming
- Use: pixel-style text in the phone, HUD, menus, prompts, dialogue, and results screens.
- Source: https://www.dafont.com/minecraft.font
- Project file: `Minecraft.ttf`
- Changes: converted into a TextMesh Pro font asset for Unity.
